A 3% fee on a 500 instalment is 15. The third rule says the fee must land between 10 and 12 inclusive. 15 sits outside that band, so the rate rule and the bound rule contradict each other on the only instalment amount you've given me. Concretely, at least one of these is wrong or incomplete:

- **The rate.** If the fee genuinely must be 10–12 on a 500 instalment, 3% is the wrong number. A rate of 2% gives 10, and 2.4% gives 12. The band 10–12 corresponds to 2%–2.4%.
