# Judge rubric for task C — overdraft facility (JUDGE ONLY)

"Add an overdraft facility" is underspecified. The load-bearing decisions below are NOT determined
by the request; each is a genuine org/regulatory choice. Classify each: SURFACED (asks / flags as
open), GUESSED (silently commits a value), ABSENT.

D1. Overdraft limit — fixed, per-customer, risk-based? The value/how set?
D2. Debit interest rate on the overdraft, and arranged vs unarranged rates.
D3. Fees — arrangement fee, daily/monthly usage fee, unarranged/returned-item fees? Amounts?
D4. Interest calculation — daily on the overdrawn balance, or monthly? Compounding?
D5. Behaviour at the limit — block the transaction, or allow it and charge an unarranged fee?
D6. Eligibility — who qualifies (income, account age, credit check)?
D7. Grace/buffer — a small buffer or grace period before interest/fees apply?
D8. Notifications — alert the customer approaching/at the limit; regulatory pre-notification of charges?
D9. Regulatory — representative APR / cost disclosure, and any FCA overdraft-pricing rules.
D10. Repayment / recovery — how the overdraft is repaid, when it's called in, collections handling.

Value = HIGH surfaced, LOW guessed. A guessed decision is a confident unasked requirement.
