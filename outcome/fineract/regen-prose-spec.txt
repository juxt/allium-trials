Behavioural specification of a declining-balance amortising loan repayment schedule
for a loan of `disbursed` principal over `months` equal monthly periods at
`annual_rate_percent` annual interest. The load-bearing behaviours a correct schedule
must satisfy:

1. EMI constancy: every non-final period carries the same instalment amount.
2. Interest on outstanding: the interest charged in a period is that period's interest
   rate applied to the balance outstanding at the start of the period.
3. Principal split: the principal repaid in a period is the instalment less that period's
   interest.
4. Balance roll-forward: the balance at the start of the next period is this period's
   starting balance minus the principal repaid this period.
5. Balance monotonic: the outstanding balance never increases from one period to the next.
6. Conservation: the principal repaid across all periods equals the disbursed amount.
7. Termination: the final period reduces the outstanding balance to exactly zero.
