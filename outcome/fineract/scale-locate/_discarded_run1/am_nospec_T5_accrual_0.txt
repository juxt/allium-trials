## The difference in timing

Interest income hits the P&L (the `INTEREST_ON_LOANS` income account) at a **different transaction event** in each processor:

- **Cash processor**: income is booked only when a **repayment is received**.
- **Accrual processor**: income is booked when interest **accrues** (a periodic accrual transaction), decoupled from cash. A later repayment then just clears a receivable, touching no income account.

Both implement `AccountingProcessorForLoan` and are selected per loan product's accounting rule.

## Cash: income recognised on repayment

`CashBasedAccountingProcessorForLoan.createJournalEntriesForLoanRepayments()`. On repayment, the interest portion is credited straight to the income account:

- `CashBasedAccountingProcessorForLoan.java:756-759` — credit `INTEREST_ON_LOANS` (income) for the interest paid.
- `CashBasedAccountingProcessorForLoan.java:839-840` — the matching debit to `FUND_SOURCE`.

So the entry is Dr FUND_SOURCE / Cr INTEREST_ON_LOANS (income) at the moment cash arrives. There is no accrual step in the cash processor.

## Accrual: income recognised on accrual, receivable cleared on repayment

Two separate transaction types do the work:

1. **Accrual transaction** books the income. `AccrualBasedAccountingProcessorForLoan.createJournalEntriesForAccruals()`:
   - `AccrualBasedAccountingProcessorForLoan.java:2036-2039` — Dr `INTEREST_RECEIVABLE` / Cr `INTEREST_ON_LOANS` (income). This is where income reaches the ledger, independent of any payment. (Lines 2032-2035 handle the reversal for an accrual adjustment.)

2. **Repayment transaction** does *not* touch income. `AccrualBasedAccountingProcessorForLoan` handling of the interest portion of a repayment:
   - `AccrualBasedAccountingProcessorForLoan.java:1730-1739` — the interest is credited to `INTEREST_RECEIVABLE` (a balance-sheet asset), not to income. The offsetting debit is `FUND_SOURCE`.

So under accrual the repayment is Dr FUND_SOURCE / Cr INTEREST_RECEIVABLE, merely converting the receivable to cash. Income was already recognised at accrual time.
