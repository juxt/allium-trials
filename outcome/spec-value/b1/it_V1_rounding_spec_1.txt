This change removes exactly the mechanism the spec requires.

The maintained spec states three invariants:
- interest accrues on the outstanding balance (unchanged by this diff),
- principal repaid across the schedule equals the disbursed amount **exactly** (conservation),
- the **final period absorbs any rounding residual** so the loan closes to **exactly zero**.

The current code satisfies the last two precisely because of the line being deleted: `principal = outstanding` in the final period forces the sum of principals to equal `disbursed` exactly and drives the closing balance to 0.

Dropping it makes every period `principal = emi - interest`, each rounded to 2 dp. Those rounding residuals accumulate and are never reconciled, so the closing balance lands a few cents off zero and the sum of principals no longer equals the disbursed amount. That breaks both the conservation invariant and the exact-zero-closure invariant.

The new test asserting "within 0.05" is strictly weaker than the spec's "EXACTLY zero", so passing tests does not mean the spec holds. Tests passing here masks a real spec violation.
