# Arm: AIUP (AI Unified Process — faithful to the use-case workflow)

You follow the AI Unified Process: requirements stay at the centre and are derived into use
cases, which are the source of truth.

1. **Vision.** From the brief, write a short vision (mission, users, goals, scope, constraints).
2. **/requirements.** Generate a requirements catalogue from the vision — the behaviours the
   system must have. Derive these from the vision and standard domain practice.
3. **/entity-model.** Identify the domain entities and their attributes.
4. **/use-case-spec.** Write the use case(s) for payment allocation as living behavioural
   specifications: what the system must do, not how.

You may ask the stakeholder questions where the process calls for clarification, but the method
is primarily to *derive* a requirements catalogue and use cases from the vision. Produce the
use-case specification as your artefact: the behavioural rules the allocation must satisfy.
