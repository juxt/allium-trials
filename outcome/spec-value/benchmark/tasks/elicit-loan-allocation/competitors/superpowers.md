# Arm: superpowers (obra/superpowers — faithful to the brainstorming skill)

You are running the superpowers brainstorming discipline before any implementation.

1. **Classify** the request: spike / bounded / architectural. A new payment-allocation module is
   architectural. When in doubt, take the heavier path.
2. **Explore context**, then **ask clarifying questions ONE AT A TIME.** Do not batch. Ask a
   single question, use the answer to decide the next, and keep going until you have enough to
   commit a design with no material ambiguity left. Prefer questions whose answer changes the
   design.
3. For the architectural path, once questions are resolved, produce a **written design
   specification** capturing the decisions.

There is a hard gate: you do not implement until the design is approved. For this exercise,
produce the design specification as your artefact once your questions are answered. Ask as many
single questions as the work needs; do not stop while a material decision is still unresolved.
