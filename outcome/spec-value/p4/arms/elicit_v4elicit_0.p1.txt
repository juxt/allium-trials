## The blocking decision: "equal instalments" cannot coexist with exact payoff under penny rounding

The request asserts two things that pull against each other once money is rounded to the penny:

- Every monthly instalment is **equal** (stated).
- The loan **fully repays**: closing balance after the final instalment is exactly zero (implied by "repays it").

The exact level payment is `L = P·r / (1 − (1+r)⁻ⁿ)`. Rounded to whole pence, `L × months` almost never equals `principal + total interest` exactly. So "all instalments equal to the penny" **and** "balance lands exactly on zero" **and** "amounts are whole pence" is over-constrained and generally unsatisfiable.

I will not silently make the last instalment different to paper over this, because that is precisely the reconciliation this process forbids. Encoded literally (an `invariant` that all instalment amounts are equal, plus a `requirement` that a schedule exists closing the balance to exactly zero in whole pence), `allium analyse` reports this pair CONTRADICTORY, or the exact-payoff requirement INFEASIBLE. That is the spec biting as intended.

**Question 1 (OPEN, cannot guess):** When the exact level payment does not divide into whole pence, which constraint gives?

- (b) Keep all instalments equal and accept the closing balance within a stated **rounding tolerance** (not exactly zero).
