## The one decision that dominates the rest

"Equal monthly instalments over a fixed term" does **not** pin down the loan. Two different products both produce equal instalments from `(disbursed, annual_rate_pct, months)`:

- **Reducing-balance (amortising):** interest each month accrues on the outstanding balance; the level instalment comes from the annuity formula `A = P·i / (1 − (1+i)⁻ⁿ)`. Early instalments are mostly interest, later ones mostly principal.
- **Flat / add-on interest:** interest is computed once on the original principal (`P · annual_rate · years`), added to principal, and the total is split evenly. Every instalment carries the same interest and the same principal.

Both give you equal monthly payments, so the phrasing in the brief is satisfied either way, and they produce materially different schedules and different total interest for the same inputs. Given this product line's history with flat and add-on interest, I will not assume reducing-balance. This is the first thing I need decided, and it changes almost every constraint below.
