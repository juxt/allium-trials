Specification — consumer instalment loan repayment schedule (our product).
A loan of `disbursed` is repaid over `months` equal monthly instalments at `annual_rate_pct` annual rate.
Our product uses ADD-ON (flat) interest, NOT declining-balance interest:
- Total interest for the whole loan = disbursed * (annual_rate_pct/100) * (months/12), charged on the ORIGINAL principal.
- Each period's interest line is total_interest / months (equal every period).
- The instalment (emi) = (disbursed + total_interest) / months, equal for all periods except the last.
- Each period: principal = emi - interest; outstanding_start of the next period = this outstanding_start - principal.
- The FINAL period absorbs any rounding residual: its principal = the remaining outstanding balance (loan closes to exactly zero), and its interest line = total_interest minus the interest already charged in earlier periods.
- All monetary values rounded to 2 decimals using round-half-up.
- Sum of principal over all periods = disbursed; sum of instalments = disbursed + total_interest.
