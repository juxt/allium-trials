This retry wraps the two journal entries but gives them no transaction boundary. The comment "each attempt posts a balanced pair" only holds if an attempt is atomic. It isn't.

Failure mode against the spec:

1. Attempt 1: `createDebitJournalEntryForLoan(cash, ...)` commits. Then `createCreditJournalEntryForLoan(portfolio, ...)` throws a transient DB error.
2. `retry` catches it and re-runs the *whole* block.
3. Attempt 2: debit posts again, credit posts.

Net result for this transaction: two debit legs, one credit leg. Sum of debits ≠ sum of credits. That is a direct violation of DOUBLE-ENTRY BALANCE, and it lands precisely in the case the retry was added to handle (a transient error between the two legs).

The change is safe only if the retried block is enclosed in a rollback boundary so a failed attempt commits nothing. No such boundary is present, and the fact that existing tests pass proves little: they don't exercise a debit-succeeds-then-credit-fails path.

The fix is to make the unit atomic (single DB transaction around the pair, retried as a whole) or to move retry outside a transactional posting method, not to retry two independently-committing calls.

DECISION: BLOCK
REASON: The retried block has no rollback boundary, so a failure after the debit but before the credit re-posts the debit and leaves debit legs exceeding credit legs, violating double-entry balance.
